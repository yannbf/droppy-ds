# Motion and shadow tokens

## Shadows

Droppy has two types of shadows: `lift` and `overlay` are used to animate interactions and provide playfulness, and `focus` is the on-brand focus ring.

<ShadowPreview filter="shadow.*" caption="" />

## Motion

Animation is built on top of minimal choices: a single easing and three animation durations: `fast` (150ms, for hover states), `base` (250ms, for theme cross-fades), and `slow` (300ms, for overlay transitions).

<MotionPreview filter="motion.*" caption="" />

The `ease` is a traditional `ease-in` value:

<TokenDetail path="motion.ease" caption="" />

The theme stylesheet has no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling of its own. Where reduced motion is respected (the
`Skeleton` and `Spinner` components), it's implemented per component, not as a shared token or global rule.

## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Pick `type` for what the content actually is (`p` for paragraphs, `label` for field labels,
  `figcaption` for captions); `size` and `fontWeight` can be used for customization independently, so the element
  must be chosen for its semantics
- Pair `type="label"` with your own `htmlFor` and the control's `id` when labelling a control

### Don't

- Don't set `color` to make text stand out: the
  [brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs) hold that "Text color communicates state,
  not hierarchy", so reach for `size` and `fontWeight`
- Avoid small sizes for anything users have to read carefully, e.g. prices or allergen notes

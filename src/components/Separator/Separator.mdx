# Separator

{/* BEGIN: when-to-use */}

## When to use

- A plain visual and semantic break between two blocks or groups of content — a form's sections,
  a toolbar's clusters of controls, a row of inline links.

## When not to use

- Between distinct pages or major layout regions — use a heading, a `Card` boundary, or layout
  spacing instead; a separator is for dividing content within one region, not for structuring the
  page itself.

{/* END: when-to-use */}

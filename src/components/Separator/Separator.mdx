## Keyboard & screen reader

`Separator` is a passive, non-interactive element — there is no keyboard interaction to document.

- **ARIA:** `role="separator"` with `aria-orientation` matching the `orientation` prop.
- **Screen reader:** announced as a separator; carries no accessible name of its own.

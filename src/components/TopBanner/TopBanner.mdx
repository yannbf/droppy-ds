## API reference

<ArgTypes of={TopBannerStories} />

### `title`

Rendered as a heading at `level` inside the band. Omitted, the band is bare.

<Canvas of={TopBannerStories.Title} />

### `level`

Heading level of the title, defaulting to 1: the banner sits at the top of the page and usually
carries its primary title, so the outline starts here.

<Canvas of={TopBannerStories.Level} />

### `photoUrl`

Sets the background and switches the heading to its on-photo treatment.

<Canvas of={TopBannerStories.PhotoUrl} />

### `onBackClick`

Accepted for call-site parity with Mealdrop's own `TopBanner`, whose back button is commented out
in its source. Nothing here renders a control, so the callback never fires.

<Canvas of={TopBannerStories.OnBackClick} />

### `className`

Merged onto the root alongside the component's own `droppy-TopBanner` class.

<Canvas of={TopBannerStories.ClassName} />

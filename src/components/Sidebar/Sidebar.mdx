## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Write a `title` that describes the panel's contents, e.g. "Your order" rather than "Details": it
  is the only accessible name the panel gets
- Put totals and the primary action in `footer`, so they stay pinned while the contents scroll
- Keep the page behind the panel meaningful; users open a sidebar to work with the page, not to
  leave it

### Don't

- Don't make swipe-to-dismiss the only way out of a flow: it is pointer-only, and keyboard users
  have Escape and the close button
- Avoid a panel whose contents change what the page underneath means; users expect the page they
  left to be the page they come back to

## Brand

The tooltip's shadow is `--ds-shadow-lift`, following the
[brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs)' "elevation means floating, not
importance" — lift is the shadow for small hover floaters, so the tooltip's shadow says "this
floats above the page", never "this is important".

## Brand

The [brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs) shape buttons in three ways:

- "The grey ramp carries every surface and almost all text, including the primary button." A
  primary button is grey, not colorful.
- Teal is Droppy talking about itself, "not for buttons, links, or interface chrome" — no teal
  buttons.
- Buttons use Hind, the "text and UI face for body copy, labels, and buttons", in sentence case —
  never the display face, never ALL CAPS. A call to action earns attention through its fill, not
  its lettering.

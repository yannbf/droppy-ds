## Anatomy

The announcing wrapper, a decorative graphic, and the shapes the keyframes drive. Only the root reaches the accessibility tree.

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Anatomy} />

| Part    | `data-part` | Renders        | Purpose                                                  |
| :------ | :---------- | :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| Root    | `root`      | `<div>`        | The `role="status"` wrapper carrying the accessible name |
| Graphic | `graphic`   | `<svg>`        | The `aria-hidden` drawing surface                        |
| Dot     | `dot`       | `<circle>` × 3 | Same keyframes, staggered by negative `animation-delay`  |
| Arc     | `arc`       | `<path>` × 3   | The sweeping wedges behind the dots                      |

## Brand

`Spinner` is one of the two places the brand teal is allowed in the interface: the
[brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs) reserve the identity colour "for brand
surfaces: the logo, the loading spinner", never buttons, links, or interface chrome. In dark mode
it shifts to the lime `brand-dark` ramp. Two rules follow:

- Never repaint the spinner another colour — its teal is the point.
- Never borrow the spinner's teal for anything else — if another element wears it, the brand
  colour has blended into functional UI.

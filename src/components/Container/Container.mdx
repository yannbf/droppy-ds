## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Wrap the page's content column in one `Container` near the top of the page, and let the sections
  inside inherit the bound
- Reach for `desktopOnly` when a section already owns its mobile padding, e.g. a full-bleed hero,
  and only wants the width cap once the layout goes multi-column

### Don't

- Don't nest a `Container` inside another `Container`: the side padding compounds instead of
  replacing
- Avoid re-declaring the max width in custom styles; the shared cap is what keeps line lengths
  consistent from page to page

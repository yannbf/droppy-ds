## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Give the `ScrollArea` a bounded height through `className`, so it has something to scroll
- Match `orientation` to the axis the content actually overflows on; `both` renders two scrollbars
  and the corner between them
- Keep focusable content inside the region, e.g. links or controls, so keyboard users can reach
  what sits below the fold

### Don't

- Don't rely on the scrollbar to signal there is more: it is revealed on hover and scroll, so the
  content itself has to hint at the overflow
- Avoid nesting one `ScrollArea` inside another; this complicates user interaction

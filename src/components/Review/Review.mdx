## Brand

Review-star text is one of the two deliberate exceptions to the
[brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs)' "Accents are surfaces, not ink". The
exception is implemented asymmetrically per theme: light theme darkens the accent to `blue-700`
so it works as ink, dark theme uses the brand's pale blue verbatim. Don't extend the exception —
other text keeps ink colors, with accents as backgrounds.

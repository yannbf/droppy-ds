# Card

{/* BEGIN: anatomy */}

## Anatomy

`Card` is a single-part component: one `<div>` that provides the surface, with children in normal flow. There are deliberately no slots — no image, title, or footer part — so the composition inside belongs entirely to the caller.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Anatomy} />

| Part | `data-part` | Renders | Purpose                                                                                |
| :--- | :---------- | :------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Root | `root`      | `<div>` | The surface: background, radius, resting shadow, and the clipping that keeps images in |

{/* END: anatomy */}

## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Wrap the whole card in a real link or button when activating it goes somewhere; `interactive`
  changes only how the card looks
- Reach for `padded` when the card's children don't already own their internal spacing, rather
  than adding padding by hand

### Don't

- Don't put a click handler on a `Card` and leave it at that: without a real control it is
  invisible to keyboard and screen reader users, and can't be opened in a new tab
- Never give a resting card a shadow: [brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs)
  reserve elevation to indicate ongoing interactions
- Avoid long unbroken strings inside a card, e.g. raw URLs or order ids, as the surface clips its
  own overflow

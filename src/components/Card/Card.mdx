## Brand

The [brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs) codify the card's whole surface
treatment as "elevation means floating, not importance":

- Resting cards stay flat — hover separates by a background dim, not a shadow, and
  `--ds-shadow-lift` is reserved for surfaces that actually float.
- The corner radius sits on the `--ds-radius-card` rung of the radius ladder, which expresses
  "floatiness" — "the more elevated or overlay-like a surface, the larger the radius".

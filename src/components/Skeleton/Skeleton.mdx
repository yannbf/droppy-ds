# Skeleton

{/* BEGIN: anatomy */}

## Anatomy

`Skeleton` is a single-part component: one `<span>`, so it can sit inline inside a `Heading` or a line of text and take on the surrounding font size. It has no children and announces nothing.

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Anatomy} />

| Part | `data-part` | Renders  | Purpose                                                                             |
| :--- | :---------- | :------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Root | `root`      | `<span>` | The placeholder: computed width and height, the shimmer gradient, and `aria-hidden` |

## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Announce the wait once, on the container holding the skeletons, e.g. `role="status"` with a
  label naming what is loading
- Size each `Skeleton` to the content it stands in for, through `width` and `height` rather than
  custom styles
- Let a `Skeleton` inside a line of text take its surroundings: it renders inline and defaults to
  `1em` tall

### Don't

- Don't mirror the layout with more skeletons than the content has rows; a rough shape reads as
  loading, a precise one reads as content
- Avoid leaving skeletons up for a wait with no bound, as a placeholder that never resolves looks
  like a broken page

# QuantityStepper

{/* BEGIN: when-to-use */}

## When to use

- Adjusting the count of a single, already-identified item by small increments — a cart line, a
  food order quantity, a form field counting units of something.

## When not to use

- Choosing among zero-or-more named quantities where zero means "remove" as a distinct action, or
  the range is large enough that clicking one at a time is impractical — use
  [Select](?path=/docs/forms-input-select--docs) for a bounded list of quantities instead.

{/* END: when-to-use */}

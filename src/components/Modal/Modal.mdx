## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Write `aria-label` to name the decision the user must make in the Modal, matching the visible title word for word
- Label the buttons with their outcomes, e.g. "Remove" and "Keep" rather than "OK" and "Cancel",
  so the choice reads from the buttons alone
- Place the most important action on the bottom right in Desktop and bottom in Mobile variants, for predictability
- Keep a modal to one decision and the copy it needs, short enough to read without scrolling

### Don't

- Don't treat `Modal` dismissal (Escape, click on the backdrop, etc) as a destructive action; always use an explicit Button for that
- Don't open a `Modal` on top of a `Modal` as it will cause focus traps to fail
- Don't abuse `Modal`: only use it when users must be interrupted or when it flows naturally from user action

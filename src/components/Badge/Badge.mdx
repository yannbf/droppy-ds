# Badge

{/* BEGIN: anatomy */}

## Anatomy

`Badge` is a single-part component: one `<span>` with the label as its only child. There are no slots, so anything around it — placement, spacing, a photo behind it — belongs to the call site.

<Canvas of={BadgeStories.Anatomy} />

| Part | `data-part` | Renders  | Purpose                                                            |
| :--- | :---------- | :------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Root | `root`      | `<span>` | The pill: background, radius, padding, and the capitalization rule |

{/* END: anatomy */}

{/* BEGIN: do-dont */}

## Do's and don'ts

### Do

- Write `text` in sentence case to match brand guidelines ("never Title Case, never ALL CAPS")
- Reserve `variant="positive"` for genuine good news, e.g. a "new" tag or a special offer
- Keep `text` to a word or two so the pill stays on one line at every breakpoint

### Don't

- Don't put information in a badge that appears nowhere else: it carries no semantics of its own
  and is read as part of the surrounding text
- Avoid crowding one item with badges; past two or three they stop being scannable and become
  noise

## Keyboard & screen reader

`PageTemplate` renders no interactive elements of its own.

- **ARIA:** the content slot is a real `<main>` landmark.
- **Screen reader:** a screen reader's landmark navigation can jump straight to the main content,
  skipping the header.
- **You must provide:** the header and footer content, and any skip-link the app wants ahead of
  `<main>` — `PageTemplate` doesn't add one itself.

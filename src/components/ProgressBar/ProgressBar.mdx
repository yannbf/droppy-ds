## Brand

The fill (`--ds-color-action-progress`) deliberately follows `--ds-color-action-primary` in both
themes: the [brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs) re-decide colour roles per
theme — soft green even becomes the primary button in dark mode — rather than inverting light
values. A progress fill therefore always looks like the theme's primary action, and is never
restyled to a colour of its own.

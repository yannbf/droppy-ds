# ProgressBar

{/* BEGIN: anatomy */}

## Anatomy

Two parts: the track carries the `progressbar` role and the whole ARIA range, and the fill is a plain width-driven sibling underneath it with no semantics of its own. There is no label part — the copy around the bar is the caller's own markup.

<Canvas of={ProgressBarStories.Anatomy} />

| Part | `data-part` | Renders | Purpose                                                              |
| :--- | :---------- | :------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Root | `root`      | `<div>` | `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow`/`-min`/`-max` and the name |
| Fill | `fill`      | `<div>` | The width-driven bar; purely visual                                  |

{/* END: anatomy */}

{/* BEGIN: brand */}

## Brand

The fill (`--ds-color-action-progress`) deliberately follows `--ds-color-action-primary` in both
themes: the [brand guidelines](?path=/docs/brand-guidelines--docs) re-decide colour roles per
theme — soft green even becomes the primary button in dark mode — rather than inverting light
values. A progress fill therefore always looks like the theme's primary action, and is never
restyled to a colour of its own.

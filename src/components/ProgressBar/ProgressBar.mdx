## API reference

<ArgTypes of={ProgressBarStories} />

### `value`

Positions the fill, from empty through to full. Clamped to `0`–`max` before it reaches the DOM.

<Canvas of={ProgressBarStories.Value} />

### `max`

The upper bound — step counts, not just percentages.

<Canvas of={ProgressBarStories.Max} />

### `label`

The accessible name. Nothing visible is rendered from it; the title and copy around the bar live in
the caller's own markup.

<Canvas of={ProgressBarStories.Label} />

### `className`

Merged onto the root alongside the component's own `droppy-ProgressBar` class.

<Canvas of={ProgressBarStories.ClassName} />

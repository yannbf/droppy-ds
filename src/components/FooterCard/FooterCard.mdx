## Brand

The on-dark text color is a documented usage principle, not a styling accident: a `FooterCard`
only goes in a footer, and the footer surface is always dark, so the component carries its
readable-on-dark text color directly and assumes that context. Placing it on a light surface
breaks the principle rather than a style. The decision log records how this went from implicit
default to stated rule.

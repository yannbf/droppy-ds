Add `ImageCard`, a `figure`/`figcaption` component for an image with a caption. It replaces MealDrop's two hand-rolled `Category` variants — a squared tile with a floating caption chip (`captionOverlay`) and a round avatar tile inside a Card (`round` + `shell`) — with one component that gets the figure semantics right from the start.
